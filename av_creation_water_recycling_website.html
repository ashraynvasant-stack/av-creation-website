The process

How the concept works

Four connected stages form the idea. A working prototype would need testing to confirm water quality, hygiene, and performance.

STEP 01

Collect

Drain water from the shower is directed into a collection tank.

STEP 02

Filter

Filter stages remove hair, particles, and other selected impurities.

STEP 03

Store

Treated water is held in a covered tank while the system checks its condition.

STEP 04

Circulate

A pump sends suitable water back to the shower loop; unsafe water must be diverted away.

Future roadmap

What comes next?

  • Build a small prototype to test the collection, filtration, and pumping loop.
  • Check hygiene and water quality with qualified professionals before any reuse.
  • Measure water savings against an ordinary shower.
  • Improve accessibility and consider small bathrooms, low-profile designs, and safe entry.
  • Run a pilot before considering production and wider availability.
